# Product Brief

## The learner (one user)

- Cantonese speaker based in Hong Kong.
- Spanish at roughly **B1/B2**.
- **Strong** spontaneous speaking and storytelling; oral B2 performance is already strong.
- **Weak**: grammar accuracy, reading-exam strategy, listening-exam strategy,
  formal writing, and structured written argumentation.
- Studies **mostly on a phone**, ~**15 minutes/day**, and loses focus with long
  walls of text.

## The goal

Over ~6 months, reach a **high-confidence pass level for the non-oral DELE B2
components**: reading, listening, and written expression.

We measure progress with an internal **readiness** signal — never a pass guarantee.

## What this app is

A calm, visual, card-first personal trainer that gives **one next-best task at a
time**, fits in 15 minutes, and closes every session with a structured
**Learning Receipt**.

## What this app is NOT

- Not a chatbot, not a chat-history interface.
- Not a Telegram bot or native mobile app.
- Not a content generator that invents its own syllabus.
- Not an exam-pass predictor.

## Why it should work for this learner

| Weakness | How the app targets it |
|---|---|
| Grammar accuracy | Ladder from recognition (MC) → controlled production → free writing; recurring errors are tracked and re-surfaced on schedule. |
| Reading strategy | Timed reading tasks with explicit strategy nudges (skim, scan, locate) in Cantonese. |
| Listening strategy | (Reserved for a near-term phase) timed audio tasks with pre-listening strategy. |
| Formal writing | Writing Focus editor with word bands + max 3 focused feedback points, DELE task templates. |
| Focus / phone-first | 15-min sessions, one task, progressive disclosure, no walls of text. |

## Success signals (internal, evidence-based)

- **Timed-task evidence**: accuracy and time on reading/listening under time limits.
- **Skill coverage**: how much of the fixed B2 curriculum has been touched and drilled.
- **Recurring-error transfer**: do known errors stop reappearing across contexts?
- **Mock results**: periodic timed mocks per component.

## How the AI helps the learner improve (Phase 2)

The LLM plugs in **under the code's learning requirements** (相應學習要求). It:

- explains *why* an answer is wrong, personalised to this learner's recurring errors;
- rewrites corrections and model answers at the right level;
- generates **task variants** for an existing curriculum item (never new syllabus);
- adapts explanation language/length (concise Cantonese).

It does **not** choose what to study next, score, or decide mastery — code does.
